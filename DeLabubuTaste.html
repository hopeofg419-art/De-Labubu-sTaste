Where It Began

From a Family Kitchen to Oye-Ekiti's Table

De Labubu's Taste began with a simple belief: that good food is one of the most powerful forms of love. What started as cooking for family gatherings and close friends in Oye-Ekiti quickly grew into something larger — a community gathering point where people from all walks of life could share a table.

We opened our doors in 2020 with a small menu, big hearts, and an unshakeable commitment to quality. No cutting corners. No compromise on ingredients. Every pot stirred with intention, every plate sent out with pride.

Today, De Labubu's Taste serves hundreds of customers weekly — everyday families, university students, corporate professionals and visiting guests. The food has evolved. The menu has grown. But the heart of what we do has never changed.

What Drives Us

Our Core Values

01

Freshness, Always

We shop the market every morning. We don't store food overnight to serve the next day. If it's not fresh, it doesn't leave our kitchen.

02

Community First

We are part of Oye-Ekiti. We hire locally, source locally, and give back locally. When our community thrives, we thrive.

03

Respect on Every Plate

Every customer — whether ordering a ₦500 snack or a full corporate spread — gets the same quality, the same care, and the same respect.

04

Cultural Pride

We celebrate Nigerian cuisine at its best. Egusi, jollof, pepper soup, ofada — these are not just dishes; they are identity, memory, and heritage.
